Turning to the world of crypto technology, network security is taking center stage. Industry experts are highlighting a core feature of the Ethereum network known as the inactivity leak. If a large group of network validators suddenly goes offline during a major outage, the system automatically begins burning their staked funds. It sounds harsh, but this built in penalty ensures the blockchain can heal itself and keep confirming transactions safely even during severe disruptions.
